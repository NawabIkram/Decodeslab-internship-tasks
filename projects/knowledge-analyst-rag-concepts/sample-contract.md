# Fictional Master Services Agreement

This sample contract is fictional and created only for the RAG concept demonstration.

## Page 1, Section 1.1: Parties

This Master Services Agreement is entered into by **Northstar Analytics LLC** ("Client") and **BrightPath AI Solutions Pvt. Ltd.** ("Provider"). The effective date of this Agreement is **1 July 2026**.

## Page 1, Section 1.2: Purpose

Provider will deliver artificial intelligence consulting, workflow automation, and document intelligence services for Client's internal legal operations team.

## Page 2, Section 2.1: Scope of Services

Provider will design a prototype contract analysis assistant, configure document search workflows, and prepare user training materials. Provider will not provide legal advice or act as a licensed law firm.

## Page 2, Section 2.2: Deliverables

Provider must deliver a working prototype, a user guide, and a final implementation report by **30 September 2026**. Client must provide sample contracts and feedback within five business days of each review request.

## Page 3, Section 3.1: Fees

Client will pay Provider a fixed fee of **USD 48,000** for the initial project. The fee will be paid in three installments: 40% on signing, 30% after prototype delivery, and 30% after final acceptance.

## Page 3, Section 3.2: Late Payment

Invoices unpaid after **15 days** from the invoice date may accrue interest at **1.5% per month** or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

## Page 4, Section 4.1: Confidentiality

Each party must protect the other party's confidential information using at least reasonable care. Confidential information includes contracts, business processes, customer information, pricing, technical documentation, and non-public product plans.

## Page 4, Section 4.2: Data Handling

Provider may process Client documents only for the purpose of performing the services. Provider may not use Client documents to train public models or disclose Client documents to third parties without written approval.

## Page 5, Section 5.1: Security Requirements

Provider must maintain access controls, encrypted storage, audit logs, and role-based permissions for systems that process Client documents. Provider must notify Client within **72 hours** after discovering a confirmed security incident.

## Page 5, Section 5.2: Audit Rights

Client may request one security audit per calendar year with at least **10 business days** written notice. The audit must occur during normal business hours and must not unreasonably disrupt Provider operations.

## Page 6, Section 6.1: Term and Renewal

The Agreement begins on **1 July 2026** and continues for **12 months** unless terminated earlier. The Agreement renews automatically for one-year terms unless either party gives written non-renewal notice at least **30 days** before the renewal date.

## Page 6, Section 6.2: Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate the Agreement for convenience by giving **45 days** written notice. Client must pay for all services performed and approved expenses incurred before the termination effective date.

## Page 7, Section 7.1: Limitation of Liability

Except for confidentiality breaches, data misuse, fraud, or willful misconduct, each party's total liability is limited to the fees paid or payable under this Agreement during the **six months** before the event giving rise to the claim.

## Page 7, Section 7.2: Excluded Damages

Neither party will be liable for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential damages, including lost profits or lost data, except where such damages arise from confidentiality breaches or data misuse.

## Page 8, Section 8.1: Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflict of law principles.

## Page 8, Section 8.2: Dispute Resolution

The parties must first attempt good-faith negotiation for **20 days**. If unresolved, disputes will be submitted to confidential arbitration in New York County, New York.
